House Cup & XP

Members earn XP from chatting and star reactions, feeding their house's House Cup average. Mods post the live board with /house-cup.

Enabled /configure-house-cup
Turn House Cup XP (chatting + star reactions) on or off for this server.

XP Rates

Requires Manage Server permission

Reset XP Data
Wipes every member's XP and message count back to zero, including what they've banked towards their house. This can't be undone.

Bonus Points /house-points

Each house's House Cup points are its members' average XP, plus whatever bonus you award here. Bonus points are separate from XP and don't affect any individual member's stats.

Requires Manage Server permission

Reset Bonus Points
Sets every house's bonus points back to zero. Each house's XP average is unaffected. This can't be undone.
